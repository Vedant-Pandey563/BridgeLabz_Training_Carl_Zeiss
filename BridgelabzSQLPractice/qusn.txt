Billing & Inventory System
Requirements
1) Add products with stock quantity.
2) Create a bill when a customer buys items.
3) Reduce stock automatically when a bill is created.
4) Prevent billing if stock is insufficient.
5) Calculate the bill total automatically.
6) Provide a report showing daily sales.
7) Calculate total revenue using a function.
8) Roll back the entire operation if something fails
To implement this, we must use:
* Stored Procedure
* Trigger
* View
* Function
* Subquery
* Transaction (BEGIN TRAN / COMMIT / ROLLBACK)

Step 4 — Stored Procedure with Transaction
This is the most important part.
Create Bill
The procedure will:

1 Insert Bill
2 Insert BillItems
3 Reduce stock
4 Calculate total

Why Transaction is Important

Example:
The customer buys 3 products

--> Steps happening:
1 Insert Bill
2 Insert BillItems
3 Reduce stock
4 Calculate total

If step 3 fails, we must undo everything.

Transaction ensures:
All Success  → Commit
Any Failure  → RollBack
